Recovery packet — do these in order

  1. Stop contact with the number in the note. Do not call back.
  2. If you paid by card or bank, call that institution now and freeze the payment method you used.
  3. If you installed remote-access software they asked for, uninstall it and disconnect the device from the internet until you have.
  4. Change your PayPal password and turn on two-factor authentication.
  5. Report the invoice in PayPal (Activity → the invoice → Report) and keep the original message — do not delete it.

    What Buyer Protection does — and does not — cover

    • It covers eligible Goods & Services payments only. Friends & Family payments are never covered.
    • You must open a dispute within 180 days of the payment date.
    • Not covered: real estate; motorized vehicles; custom-made or one-off items; gift cards, prepaid cards and other cash equivalents; donations; financial products and investments; items collected in person or paid for in cash; industrial machinery; anything that violates PayPal policy.
    • “Significantly not as described” has a specific standard — an item that works but disappoints may not qualify.
    • You may have to return the item at your own cost before a refund.

    Goods & Services is protected with conditions — never a universal guarantee.
