What the suite does — in plain words

A · WITNESS fake-invoice guardian

Paste the invoice email. WITNESS pulls out what it claims — amount, seller, invoice ID, the phone number in the note — then looks the invoice up in the account and reads 31 days of real transactions. If it isn't there, you owe nothing, and the suite can cancel the invoice for you. No live account check, no verdict: the answer is forced to UNVERIFIED.

B · SEATBELT Friends & Family → protected

Paste the seller chat. SEATBELT quotes the exact lines that pressure you — urgency, secrecy, “friends only” — and rates the risk. Then one click creates and sends a real Goods & Services invoice, fee math included, so the payment lands on the rail Buyer Protection actually covers.

C · AUDITOR history X-ray

One read of 180 days of history, one report: subscriptions you're still paying for, charges that hit twice, prices that crept up, and your true totals — fees and currency-conversion spread included. If the history can't be pulled, the X-ray fails loudly instead of quietly showing you sample data.

How it protects you — and where the protection stops

It checks the account, not the story

Scam invoices are written to sound real. WITNESS doesn't grade the writing — it asks PayPal what actually happened, and says UNVERIFIED when it couldn't ask.

Every call is stamped

LIVE SANDBOX means a real call hit the sandbox account. SIMULATED means a labeled preview. The receipts page stores every call — honesty is kept, not just displayed.

Protection has conditions

Friends & Family has NO Buyer Protection. Goods & Services disputes must be opened within 180 days. Goods & Services is protected with conditions — never a universal guarantee, and this page won't pretend otherwise.
