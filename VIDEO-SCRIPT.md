## Scenes

**S1 · 0:00–0:20 — Landing hero, slow push-in on the name**
> Every day, PayPal buyers get invoices that look real — and aren't.
> The scam works because nothing checks the story before the money
> moves. This is PayPal Protection Suite: three agents that work for
> the buyer. Not the seller. You.

**S2 · 0:20–0:32 — Carousel glides across the three agents**
> WITNESS checks the invoice. SEATBELT keeps the deal on the protected
> rail. AUDITOR X-rays your history. Watch them work.

**S3 · 0:32–1:08 — WITNESS: paste → live lookup → verdict → decline**
> An invoice lands: Norton renewal, four ninety-nine ninety-nine —
> for an antivirus I never bought. I paste it into WITNESS. It reads
> what the email claims — then it asks PayPal, not the email. The
> invoice is looked up in the real account, across thirty-one days of
> transactions. No match. No charge. And if it can't check the
> account, WITNESS is not allowed to guess: the verdict is forced to
> UNVERIFIED. One click — the invoice is declined, and its status is
> re-read to prove it.

**S4 · 1:08–1:44 — SEATBELT: pressure chat → risk → conversion**
> Now a seller chat: "Send it Friends and Family. I don't cover fees.
> Do it now, before it's gone." SEATBELT quotes the pressure lines
> back and rates the risk — because Friends and Family has NO Buyer
> Protection. Then one click converts the deal: a real Goods and
> Services invoice, created and sent by the suite, fee math done by
> code — the buyer protected, the seller netting exactly the price
> they asked.

**S5 · 1:44–2:16 — AUDITOR: X-ray cards land one by one**
> AUDITOR reads a hundred eighty days of history in one pass. The
> subscription I forgot I had. The charge that hit twice. The price
> that crept up forty percent. And what currency conversion quietly
> took. Every figure is computed by code — the AI only explains it.
> And if the history can't be pulled, the X-ray fails loudly. It
> never swaps in sample data and calls it yours.

**S6 · 2:16–2:34 — Receipts page: the stamps**
> Every call the suite makes is kept as a receipt — stamped LIVE when
> real account data backed it, SIMULATED when it didn't. Honesty
> isn't a claim here. It's a log.

**S7 · 2:34–2:48 — Pull back to the hero; name + repo URL**
> Three agents. One side: yours. PayPal Protection Suite — it checks
> the story before your money moves.

## Production notes

- The VO is written for the LIVE version — record/build after the
  sandbox keys are seated and the spike has run, so the receipts on
  screen say LIVE SANDBOX.
- Every claim in the script is something the app demonstrably does
  (live wiring landed 2026-11-11; honesty coercion is code, not copy).
- VO delivery is Carlos's call: he reads it, or a generated voice
  reads it. The Framer build times to the script either way.
- Never on camera: the `.env`, keys, or a real PayPal login.
